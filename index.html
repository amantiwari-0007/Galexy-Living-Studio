A FEW HELPFUL ANSWERS

Good to know.

Have a question before you enquire? Start here, or send us your specific requirements.

Ask us something else
What products can I enquire about?

You can ask about mattresses, pillows, bedding, bedsheets, curtains, blinds and other home-textile requirements. Choose the closest option in the enquiry form and add details in your message.

Can I ask about custom sizes or curtain stitching?

Yes. Include approximate measurements, the room, preferred style and any fabric or finishing ideas. Final measurements, availability and scope should be confirmed with the studio.

What should I include in my enquiry?

Your name and phone number are required. If you can, also share the product you are interested in, your location, approximate budget, timeline, measurements and any inspiration or colour preferences.

How will I send my enquiry?

When you submit the website form, it prepares your details for WhatsApp. Please review the message and press Send in WhatsApp to actually send it. The website does not yet provide a central online enquiry database.
